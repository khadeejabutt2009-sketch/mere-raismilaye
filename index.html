BEAUTY THAT LIES WITHIN

If ever I call you beautiful,
Never think I speak of your face,
Nor of your eyes, nor features
That time may one day erase.

When I call you beautiful,
I may mean your heart within,
Or the soul that shines so quietly,
The beauty no mirror can begin.

For we never choose how we are made,
Nor the face that we are given,
But we choose the heart we carry,
And the soul by which we're driven.

So write your soul with kindness,
Let every deed leave light,
For beauty is not what the eyes can see,
But what makes the darkness bright.

We cannot choose the canvas
On which our earthly story is drawn,
But we can paint our hearts with love
From dusk until the dawn.

So make your soul a masterpiece,
Let goodness be its art,
For your Rab has already painted you
Now beautifully write your heart.

— — —

This one's for my beautiful friend.

I would dedicate this entirely to you. 🖤
